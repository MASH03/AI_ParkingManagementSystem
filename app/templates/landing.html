Dual-Agent AI Architecture

How ParkSmart AI Operates

Not a chatbot. A governed multi-agent feedback pipeline that converts anonymous operational facts into validated, human-approved actions.

1
Agent 1

Operations Analyst

Consumes privacy-minimized counts, traffic buckets, and maintenance alerts. It strictly analyzes evidence—never guesses, hallucinates, or issues direct commands.

Schema: OperationsAnalysis
2
Agent 2

Recommendation Engine

Reads the verified snapshot and Analyst findings. Produces concrete, priority-ranked actions for park rangers and dispatch. Can ask for one focused clarification loop.

Schema: RecommendationBatch
3
Human Gatekeeper

Administrator Review

Every AI recommendation stays in Pending status until a human administrator approves, modifies, or rejects it. No autonomous changes to live operations.
